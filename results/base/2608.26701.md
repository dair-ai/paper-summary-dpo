**Curator’s take**  
Co‑Scientist is a Gemini‑based multi‑agent system that moves from purely in‑silico hypothesis generation to an execution‑grounded research partner. The authors—Google DeepMind, Duke, Columbia, Google Research, and Texas A&M—show that the system can design and run experiments in materials science, biology, and computer science, and that its end‑to‑end paper‑generation pipeline reduces hallucination and plagiarism.

**Key points**

- **Materials‑science synthesis** – Co‑Scientist interfaced with a semi‑automated CVD reactor to design a non‑hazardous precursor route (C₂Cl₆) for Ti₃C₂Tₓ MXene growth. The top‑ranked recipe, executed by human experts, produced 2D layered structures that “share key structural similarities with the Ti₃C₂Tₓ MXene lattice,” though atomic confirmation is pending. Using Gemini 3 Deep Think, the system also achieved single‑attempt growth of monolayer MoS₂, MoSe₂, and WS₂ within minutes, tailoring recipes to laboratory constraints.

- **Biology phenotypic prediction** – With iterative expert refinement, Co‑Scientist built a vision pipeline that predicts emergent swarming phenotypes of engineered E. coli across IPTG gradients (0–10 mM) from sparse imaging data. The predictions “largely match unpublished wet‑lab morphological measurements,” suggesting a potential to reduce experimental screening cycles.

- **Computer‑science architecture discovery** – Fully autonomously, Co‑Scientist discovered an inference‑time scaling architecture that outperformed six frontier models on HealthBench Hard and Professional, and achieved a “significant, though modest, reduction in potential clinical harm under blinded physician evaluation.”

- **Scientific‑integrity evaluation** – A double‑blind study with 30 domain experts and 450 independent reviews showed that Co‑Scientist’s log‑based verification and safety modules “consistently reduce hallucination and plagiarism” compared to unconstrained baseline systems.

- **Why it matters** – The work demonstrates a generalizable, execution‑grounded AI research framework that can orchestrate discovery across diverse experimental domains while maintaining verifiability, moving agentic AI from ideation toward real‑world scientific acceleration.
