**Curator's take**  
JIT‑Agent tackles the problem of scaling agent harness design by training a meta‑agent that generates, repairs, and evolves task‑specific harnesses on the fly for any off‑the‑shelf agentic LLM. The authors propose a four‑module harness protocol, a unified codebase (HARNES​S FACTORY), and a three‑stage training pipeline that endows the model with adaptivity, reliability, and evolvability.

**Key points**

- **Problem & Motivation** – Agent capability depends on both the foundation model and its harness. Existing ahead‑of‑time (AOT) harnesses are brittle and hard to generalize across heterogeneous tasks, motivating a just‑in‑time (JIT) approach that tailors the harness to each task.

- **Model‑as‑a‑Harness** – JIT‑Agent receives a task specification, a fixed four‑module protocol (memory, planning, action, capability), an executable tool/skill registry, and prior harness context, then emits an executable harness that wraps any off‑the‑shelf agentic LLM.

- **Three‑Stage Training** –  
  1. **Adaptivity**: Stage I teaches task‑conditioned customization from teacher‑generated protocol‑compliant examples.  
  2. **Reliability**: Stage II converts failed generations into bounded repair trajectories, supervising recovery from compiler errors, interface mismatches, and runtime failures.  
  3. **Evolvability**: Stage III uses Evolutionary Group‑Decoupled Policy Optimization (Evo‑GDPO) to optimize harness proposals that surpass the current archive frontier while normalizing reward, latency, and cost.

- **Benchmarks & Results** – Across nine benchmarks, JIT‑Agent‑27B strengthens backbones: DeepSeek‑V4‑Flash outperforms GPT‑5.6 by +9.1 on DeepSearchQA and +4.3 on OdysseyBench; GLM‑5.2 gains up to +20.2 points. Generated harnesses remain competitive with mature runtimes such as OpenCode and Claude Code and transfer across DeepSeek V4, Mimo‑V2.5, and Qwen3.6.

- **Why it matters** – The paper demonstrates that harness intelligence can be trained as a transferable, compounding dimension of agent capability, shifting harness engineering from manual, task‑specific artifacts to a scalable, just‑in‑time synthesis paradigm.
