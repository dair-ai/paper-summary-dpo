**Curator's take**  
The paper tackles the problem of realistic user simulation for training interactive language agents.  
Hoang Phan et al. from Meta Superintelligence Labs propose MIMESIS, a 4B/9B‑parameter user simulator trained with explicit reasoning supervision and 13 behavioral patterns, and a two‑stage training pipeline that freezes the simulator to train agents via multi‑turn reinforcement learning with a new Coached On‑Policy Self‑Distillation (CSD) signal.

**Key points**

- **Simulator quality** – MIMESIS‑9B achieves a SOUL‑Index of 65.7, surpassing the strongest frontier model (GPT‑5.5, Claude‑Opus‑5, Gemini‑3.8‑Flash).  
- **Behavioral fidelity** – Compared with Claude‑Opus‑5 on RealUserSim, MIMESIS improves behavioral fidelity by 13.4 points and reduces Turing distance by 3.6 points on SimulatorArena.  
- **Training benefit** – Agents trained with the frozen MIMESIS simulator outperform those trained with GPT‑5.5 on all nine unseen evaluation user simulators across eight environments, indicating stronger generalization.  
- **Coached Self‑Distillation** – CSD uses simulator‑generated private reasoning traces and subsequent utterances to produce dense, token‑level coaching signals, yielding further gains under all nine evaluation users while the deployed agent only observes public dialogue.
