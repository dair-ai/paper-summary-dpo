**Curator's take**  
Zhenyu Zhao and Roy Zhao propose a runtime‑independent architecture for long‑lived AI agents that preserves identity, memory, and executable body across changes in models, harnesses, servers, and interaction surfaces. They formalize a continuity‑bearing substrate (I, M, B) and a migration protocol, and implement it in the Enoch system.

**Key points**  
- **Agent boundary** – The persistent agent is defined as the substrate (I, M, B) that carries identity, memory, and executable body, while models, harnesses, and servers form a replaceable execution substrate.  
- **Continuity invariants** – Six invariants govern state preservation, capability changes, and continuation authority; the quiesce–checkpoint–validate–bind–rehydrate–resume protocol enforces them.  
- **Implementation** – Enoch realizes the design with reusable software bodies, private installed state, provider contracts, and fenced migration.  
- **Empirical evidence** – Operational case studies show that an instance’s identity, nonempty memory, body revision, and historical task IDs survive host, Codexto‑Claude model/harness, and chat‑surface substitutions; a cross‑host task resumes from a verified artifact checkpoint; a supervised Codex–Muse–Codex study completes five ordinary round trips and five matched same‑runtime controls on first attempts after a qualification run; a planned worker interruption recovers through the native task API with the checkpoint preserved, a stale reply rejected, and one task completion recorded.  
- **Limitations** – The results support mechanical continuity for the tested deployments and bounded workflows but do not establish behavioral equivalence, arbitrary‑task portability, general exactly‑once external effects, or unattended reliability.  
- **Why it matters** – By separating a continuity‑bearing substrate from replaceable execution and interaction bindings, the work enables long‑lived agents to migrate across models, harnesses, and servers without losing identity or memory, addressing a key lifecycle challenge for persistent AI systems.
