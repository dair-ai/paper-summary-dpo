**Curator's take**

Side Constraints (SCs) are user‑issued rules that must persist across turns, but compaction can silently drop them. Wang et al. evaluate SC retention with the C ON PACT suite and show that most compactors keep only 17% of injected SCs. They propose an SC extractor that appends constraints separately from the task summary, achieving over 90% retention without changing the compactor or LLM.

**Key points**

- **Problem.** Compaction reduces context size but often removes SCs, leading to violations such as unauthorized email deletions.
- **Evaluation.** C ON PACT tests 750 instances per compaction condition across three scenarios, plus 126 natural SCs from GitHub coding agents.
- **Results.** Current compactors retain only 17% of injected SCs on average; the best natural SC retention is 28.6%.
- **Solution.** The SC extractor keeps constraints separate from the task summary, achieving over 90% retention and 93.4–96.3% downstream compliance.
- **Why it matters.** Persistent user constraints are essential for safe, predictable LLM behavior, especially in long‑horizon or tool‑using tasks.
