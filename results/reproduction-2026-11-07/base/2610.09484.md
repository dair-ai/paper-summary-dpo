**Curator's take**

Interactive language agents need realistic user simulators for training and evaluation. Hoang Phan et al. introduce MIMESIS, a 4B/9B user simulator trained on human conversations with explicit reasoning supervision and 13 realistic behavioral patterns. They also propose Coached On‑Policy Self‑Distillation (CSD) to use the simulator’s private thoughts as dense training signals.

**Key points**

- **Behavioral fidelity.** MIMESIS‑9B scores 65.7 on the SOUL‑Index, beating the strongest frontier model. Compared with Claude‑Opus‑5, it improves behavioral fidelity by 13.4 points and reduces Turing distance by 3.6 points on RealUserSim and SimulatorArena.
- **Training advantage.** Agents trained with MIMESIS outperform those trained with GPT‑5.5 on all nine unseen evaluation user simulators across eight environments, showing stronger generalization.
- **Coached Self‑Distillation.** CSD leverages simulator‑generated private reasoning traces and subsequent utterances to produce coaching notes, which a teacher uses for token‑level supervision. This yields further gains under all nine evaluation users.
- **Why it matters.** Off‑the‑shelf LLM simulators distort task difficulty, so purpose‑built simulators like MIMESIS provide more realistic training trajectories and better calibrated evaluation.
