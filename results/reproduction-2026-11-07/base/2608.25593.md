**Curator's take**

JIT‑Agent tackles the problem that an agent’s performance depends as much on its harness as on its foundation model. The JIT‑Agent team proposes a meta‑agent that, at inference time, generates a task‑specific harness for any off‑the‑shelf agentic LLM, repairs it when synthesis fails, and evolves it using past execution feedback.

**Key points**

- **Problem & motivation.** Agent capability is jointly determined by the model and its harness. Existing harnesses are manually designed, task‑specific, and unscalable, so the authors introduce a Just‑in‑Time (JIT) approach that generates harnesses on the fly.
- **Four‑module protocol.** A harness is factored into memory, planning, action, and capability modules, and JIT‑Agent generates executable code for each module under a fixed interface.
- **Three‑stage training.** Stage I teaches adaptivity from teacher‑generated examples; Stage II teaches reliability by converting failed generations into repair trajectories; Stage III teaches evolvability with Evolutionary Group‑Decoupled Policy Optimization (Evo‑GDPO).
- **Results.** With JIT‑Agent, DeepSeek‑V4‑Flash surpasses GPT‑5.6 by +9.1 on DeepSearchQA and +4.3 on OdysseyBench, while GLM‑5.2 gains up to +20.2 points. Generated harnesses are competitive with mature runtimes such as OpenCode and Claude Code and transfer across DeepSeek V4, Mimo‑V2.5, and Qwen3.6.
