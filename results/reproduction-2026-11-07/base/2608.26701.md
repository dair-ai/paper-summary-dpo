**Curator's take**

Co‑Scientist is a Gemini‑based multi‑agent system that moves from in‑silico hypothesis generation to execution‑grounded scientific discovery. The authors extend the system with autonomous code execution, laboratory control, and safety modules, and validate it in materials science, biology and computer science.

**Key points**

- **Materials synthesis.** Co‑Scientist designed a non‑hazardous precursor route (C₂Cl₆) for Ti₃C₂Tₓ MXene growth. Human‑executed CVD produced 2D layered structures with key structural similarities to Ti₃C₂Tₓ, though atomic confirmation is pending. The system also achieved single‑attempt monolayer growth of MoS₂, MoSe₂ and WS₂ within minutes by tailoring recipes to laboratory constraints.
- **Biology.** A vision pipeline predicted emergent swarming phenotypes of engineered E. coli across an IPTG gradient (0–10 mM) from sparse imaging data. Predictions quantitatively matched unpublished wet‑lab morphological measurements, suggesting a reduction in experimental screening cycles.
- **Computer science.** Fully autonomously, Co‑Scientist discovered an inference‑time scaling architecture that outperformed six frontier models on HealthBench Hard and Professional, and reduced potential clinical harm under blinded physician evaluation.
- **Scientific integrity.** A double‑blind study with 30 domain experts and 450 reviews showed that Co‑Scientist’s reliability modules reduce severe hallucination and plagiarism compared to unconstrained baselines.
