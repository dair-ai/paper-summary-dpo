**Curator's take**

Modern LLM agents reason and act through text, while latent reasoning is efficient but opaque and tool-inaccessible. Jiaru Zou et al. propose Hybrid Reasoning Agents, which move back and forth between latent thoughts and text in a single generation. A lightweight HybridLink bridges the two modes, and the agent's working memory keeps the whole hybrid context across turns.

**Key points**

- **HybridLink.** A residual adapter maps last-layer hidden states back into the input embedding space, letting the agent think in latent space and act through text.
- **HybridSFT.** This post-training step stabilizes tool-integrated hybrid generation by teaching the backbone to coordinate with HybridLink.
- **HybridRL.** Policy optimization over hybrid trajectories learns when to think in latent space and when to act through text.
- **Results.** Across 3 model scales and 11 benchmarks in math, science, medicine, search and agentic reasoning, HybridAgent improves performance by 10.4% on average. It cuts token usage by 35.5% to 54.7%, speeds inference by 1.8x to 2.6x and cuts cost by 2.7x to 3.7x.
- **Why it matters.** Latent thoughts become inspectable pseudo-text, so the agent keeps tool access and reasoning transparency while gaining efficiency.
