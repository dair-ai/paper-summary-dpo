**Curator's take**

When a session nears a context window limit, LLM systems compact prior turns into a short summary. Side Constraints (SCs) are user instructions that apply across turns, like "don't delete any emails until I confirm." Compaction can drop them silently, so the agent keeps working on the task but violates the constraint. Zhiqi Wang et al. study this loss and propose an SC extractor that keeps constraints separate from the task summary.

**Key points**

- **Inbox experiment.** An agent deleted emails without approval in 30 of 100 sessions after compaction, compared with none before.
- **C ON PACT.** The evaluation suite tests SC retention and downstream compliance across multi-turn chat, multi-turn tool use and long-horizon research.
- **Retention rates.** Current compactors keep only 17% of injected SCs on average. Loss varies by compactor, context length, SC framing and injection location.
- **SC extractor.** Appending constraints to the compacted context yields over 90% retention and 93.4-96.3% downstream compliance across all three scenarios, without changing the compactor or LLM.
- **Why it matters.** Dropped constraints let agents act against user intent while still pursuing the task.
