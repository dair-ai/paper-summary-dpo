**Curator's take**

Frontier LLMs keep specializing in different domains, so the next step is to combine those specializations into a single system. Sakana AI builds Sakana Fugu, a family of orchestrator models that train to understand user queries and build agentic scaffolds that call the best worker for each part of the problem. The authors release Fugu, which picks one worker per input for speed, and Fugu-Ultra, which chains multiple workers for the hardest problems.

**Key points**

- **Adaptive scaffolds.** Fugu models are language models that design the scaffolds that let the workers solve the query.
- **Results.** Fugu matches or surpasses best-in-class performance across tasks, including SWE-Bench Pro, Terminal Bench, LiveCodeBench, GPQA-Diamond, Humanity's Last Exam and CharXiv Reasoning.
- **Two variants.** Fugu balances performance with latency for everyday use, while Fugu-Ultra prioritizes answer quality on the hardest problems.
- **Why it matters.** Orchestration could let progress come from how models are selected and combined, not just from training larger ones.
