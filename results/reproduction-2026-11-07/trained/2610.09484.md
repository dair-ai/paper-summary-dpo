**Curator's take**

Training interactive agents needs real user interactions, but collecting human data is costly and hard to scale. Simulated users are cheaper, yet most simulators are built from off-the-shelf assistant LLMs, which are too cooperative and homogeneous compared with real users. Hoang Phan et al. introduce MIMESIS, a user simulator trained on human conversations with explicit reasoning supervision and 13 realistic behavioral patterns derived from real user interactions. They also propose Coached On-Policy Self-Distillation (CSD), which turns the simulator's private reasoning traces into dense token-level coaching for the agent.

**Key points**

- **SOUL-Index.** MIMESIS-9B scores 65.7, surpassing the strongest frontier model.
- **RealUserSim.** MIMESIS improves behavioral fidelity by 13.4 points over Claude-Opus-5.
- **SimulatorArena.** MIMESIS reduces Turing distance by 3.6 points over Claude-Opus-5.
- **Agent training.** Across eight environments, agents trained with MIMESIS beat those trained with GPT-5.5 under all nine unseen evaluation user simulators.
- **Coaching.** CSD adds gains under all nine evaluation users while the deployed agent conditions only on public dialogue.
- **Why it matters.** Better user simulators give agents training experiences that match real user difficulty, improving generalization to new users.
