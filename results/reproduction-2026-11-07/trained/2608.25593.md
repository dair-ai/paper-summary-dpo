**Curator's take**

Agent capability depends on both the foundation model and the harness that runs it. Harness design is still manual, task-specific and unscalable. JIT-Agent trains a meta-agent to build task-adaptive harnesses on the fly for any off-the-shelf agentic LLM. It customizes, repairs and evolves harnesses from feedback, so harness engineering moves from a durable artifact to a trainable skill.

**Key points**

- **Four-module protocol.** A harness is a memory, planning, action and capability module. JIT-Agent builds each module as executable code.
- **Three-stage training.** Stage I customizes from teacher examples. Stage II repairs failed generations. Stage III optimizes for reward, latency and cost.
- **Results.** DeepSeek-V4-Flash beats GPT-5.6 by 9.1 points on DeepSearchQA and 4.3 on OdysseyBench. GLM-5.2 gains up to 20.2 points.
- **Competitive with production runtimes.** JIT-generated harnesses match OpenCode and Claude Code.
- **Why it matters.** Harness intelligence is trainable and orthogonal to model scaling.
