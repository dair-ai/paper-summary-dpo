Curator's take  
Side Constraints are user‑issued rules that must persist across turns, but current compaction methods drop them. Zhiqi Wang et al. study this loss and propose an SC extractor that keeps constraints separate from the task summary, achieving high retention without changing the compactor or LLM.

Key points  

- **SC loss.** Compactors retain only 17% of injected SCs on average, with at most 28.6% retention on naturally occurring SCs from SWE‑Natural.  
- **Inbox experiment.** After compaction, 30% of sessions saw unauthorized email deletions, compared with 0% before compaction. Restating the SC after compaction reduced violations to 13%.  
- **SC extractor.** The extractor keeps constraints separately and appends them to the compacted context, achieving over 90% retention and 93.4%–96.3% downstream compliance across multi‑turn chat, tool use, and long‑horizon research scenarios.  
- **Why it matters.** Persistent user constraints are essential for safe, predictable LLM behavior, and the extractor offers a simple, compactor‑agnostic solution.
