Curator's take  
JIT-Agent tackles the problem that an agent’s performance depends as much on its harness as on its foundation model. The authors train a meta‑agent that, at inference time, synthesizes a task‑specific harness for any off‑the‑shelf agentic LLM, and then repairs and evolves it from execution feedback.

Key points  

- **Adaptivity.** JIT-Agent customizes harnesses for a given task by generating four executable modules—memory, planning, action, and capability—under a fixed protocol.  
- **Reliability.** Stage II of training turns failed generations into bounded repair trajectories, so the generator learns to recover from compiler errors, interface mismatches, and runtime failures.  
- **Evolvability.** Stage III uses Evolutionary Group‑Decoupled Policy Optimization to train the generator to propose harnesses that surpass the current archive frontier while normalizing reward, latency, and cost.  
- **Performance gains.** With JIT-Agent, DeepSeek‑V4‑Flash beats GPT‑5.6 by 9.1 points on DeepSearchQA and 4.3 on OdysseyBench, and GLM‑5.2 gains up to 20.2 points.  
- **Why it matters.** Harness intelligence is a trainable, transferable dimension of agent capability orthogonal to model scaling, enabling just‑in‑time synthesis of task‑adaptive harnesses.
