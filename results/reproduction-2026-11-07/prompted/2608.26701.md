**Curator's take**

Co-Scientist extends a Gemini-based multi-agent system into an execution-grounded research partner that spans hypothesis generation, experiment design, and manuscript writing. The authors demonstrate the system across materials science, biology, and computer science, showing that it can design safe CVD protocols, predict E. coli swarming phenotypes, and autonomously discover a HealthBench‑winning inference-time architecture.

**Key points**

- **Materials synthesis.** Co-Scientist interfaced with a semi-automated CVD reactor to design a non-hazardous precursor route (C₂Cl₆) for Ti₃C₂Tₓ MXene growth. Physical execution of the top-ranked recipe yielded 2D layered structures with key structural similarities to Ti₃C₂Tₓ MXene, though atomic confirmation is pending. The system also achieved single-attempt growth of monolayer MoS₂, MoSe₂, and WS₂ semiconductors, tailoring recipes to laboratory constraints in minutes.

- **Biology.** With domain experts refining the task framing, Co-Scientist built a vision pipeline that predicts emergent swarming phenotypes of engineered E. coli across IPTG concentration gradients from sparse imaging data. Predictions quantitatively matched unpublished wet-lab morphological measurements, suggesting a potential to reduce experimental screening cycles.

- **Computer science.** Fully autonomously, Co-Scientist discovered an inference-time scaling architecture that outperformed six frontier models on HealthBench Hard and Professional, and reduced potential clinical harm under blinded physician evaluation.

- **Scientific integrity.** A double-blind study with 30 domain experts and 450 independent reviews showed that Co-Scientist’s reliability modules reduce severe hallucination and plagiarism compared to unconstrained baseline systems.

- **Why it matters.** The work demonstrates a generalizable research framework that orchestrates discovery across diverse experimental surfaces while maintaining experimental verifiability, moving AI-assisted science closer to real-world, scalable impact.
