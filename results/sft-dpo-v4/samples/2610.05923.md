**Curator's take**

Competent agents need precise, verifiable environments that are resumable at any stage and evolve from observable evidence. Most long-horizon work scores only the outcome, so environments lack stage-level evidence. Junqi Liu et al. present VERA, which builds such environments from initial trajectories. A coding agent writes binary rubrics, an agent judge verifies each sandbox, and only those that pass enter the training bank. The model trains with rubric rewards or edits the harness skills, and a verifier gates checkpoints and edits with explicit acceptance criteria.

**Key points**

- **Open-source corpus.** VERA releases 9,000+ verifiable long-horizon environments.
- **Co-evolution.** A 9B model paired with its co-evolved agent beats the strongest baseline by 10.3 and 13.0 points in the two domains.
- **27B results.** At 27B it reaches 71.6 on AutoCoWorkBench and 80.7 on AutoMedBench, surpassing Claude Opus 4.8 on the former and coming within 1.2 points on the latter.
- **Transfer.** It transfers to unseen workflows on Automation-Bench and AgentClinic.
- **Why it matters.** Precise, verifiable environments let agents improve from what they actually did, not from tests.
