**Curator's take**

GPU kernel benchmarks have driven progress in AI-generated kernel optimization, but no equivalent exists for TPUs. Arya Tschand et al. present JAXBench, a TPU-native benchmark suite for AI-generated kernel optimization on Google Cloud TPUs. It contains 50 JAX workloads that are relevant to modern LLM training and inference and leave room for improvement.

**Key points**

- **Workloads.** JAXBench includes 17 production ML operators from MaxText LLMs such as Llama-3.1, DeepSeekV3, Mixtral, Mamba-2 and AlphaFold2, and 33 fused operators translated from KernelBench. Eight of the production operators ship with hand-optimized Pallas kernels from Tokamax.
- **Expert baseline.** The hand-tuned kernels are block-size tuned to establish an expert upper-bound baseline.
- **Context matters.** Conditioning on curated TPU documentation raises per-sample correctness from 5.8% to 37.3% and solves 48 of 50 benchmarks at a 1.28x geomean speedup.
- **Search structure.** Autocomp's beam-search pipeline reaches a 1.36x geomean speedup over XLA on the full suite.
- **Hand-tuned kernels.** On the 8 hand-tuned kernels, Autocomp reaches 1.60x geomean over XLA, recovering most of the 2.08x Tokamax upper bound but trailing on the specialized paged and ragged attention operators.
- **Why it matters.** High-quality TPU kernel optimization remains a challenging task, and the authors release JAXBench, its evaluation harness and baseline results to support open source contributions.
