**Curator's take**

Interactive agents need multi-turn training, but real user interactions are hard to scale. Off-the-shelf LLMs used as simulators are too helpful or too homogeneous, so agents trained on them do not match real user success rates. Hoang Phan et al. propose MIMESIS, a user simulator trained on human conversations with explicit reasoning supervision and 13 realistic behavioral patterns. They also freeze the simulator and train agents with it, adding Coached On-Policy Self-Distillation (CSD) that turns the simulator's private thoughts into dense token-level coaching.

**Key points**

- **SOUL-Index.** MIMESIS-9B scores 65.7, beating the strongest frontier model.
- **RealUserSim.** MIMESIS improves behavioral fidelity by 13.4 points over Claude-Opus-5.
- **SimulatorArena.** MIMESIS reduces Turing distance by 3.6 points over Claude-Opus-5.
- **Agent generalization.** Across eight environments, agents trained with MIMESIS beat those trained with GPT-5.5 under all nine unseen user simulators.
- **Coaching.** CSD adds gains under all nine evaluation users while the deployed agent conditions only on public dialogue.
- **Why it matters.** Better simulators give agents training trajectories that match real user difficulty, improving generalization.
