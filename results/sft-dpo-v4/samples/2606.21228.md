**Curator's take**

Frontier LLMs keep specializing in different domains, so the next step is to combine those specializations. Sakana AI proposes Sakana Fugu, a family of orchestrator models that train to understand user queries and build agentic scaffolds that call a team of frontier LLM agents. The orchestrator models are themselves LLMs, and they aim to reach performance beyond any single agent.

**Key points**

- **Two variants.** Fugu routes each query to a single worker, so its latency is comparable to a direct call to a frontier model. Fugu-Ultra composes workflows of multiple agents, trading latency for higher quality on the hardest problems.
- **Results.** Fugu matches and in many cases surpasses best-in-class performance across tasks. It is shoulder-to-shoulder with Fable 5 and Mythos Preview on rigorous engineering, scientific and reasoning benchmarks.
- **No export controls.** The models deliver frontier capability without the risk of export controls.
- **Why it matters.** If capability can be amplified by composing existing models, progress need not depend solely on training larger ones. Orchestration offers a more modular and accessible path, and may distribute the benefits of frontier AI more broadly.
