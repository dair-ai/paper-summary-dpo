**Curator's take**

Agent performance depends on both the foundation model and its harness, which manages memory, planning, action and tool orchestration. Harness design is manual, task-specific and unscalable. JIT-Agent trains a meta-agent to synthesize task-adaptive harnesses on the fly for any off-the-shelf agentic LLM. It customizes harnesses for a task, repairs them for reliable execution and self-evolves by distilling signals from prior configurations.

**Key points**

- **Four-module protocol.** The harness is a composable artifact of memory, planning, action and capability modules.
- **Three-stage training.** Stage I customizes from teacher examples, Stage II repairs failed generations and Stage III evolves harnesses with Evo-GDPO.
- **Results.** DeepSeekV4-Flash beats GPT-5.6 by 9.1 points on DeepSearchQA and 4.3 on OdysseyBench, while GLM-5.2 gains up to 20.2 points.
- **Competitive with production runtimes.** JIT-generated harnesses match OpenCode and Claude Code and improve DeepSeek V4, Mimo-V2.5 and Qwen3.6.
- **Why it matters.** Harness intelligence is trainable, transferable and orthogonal to model scaling.
