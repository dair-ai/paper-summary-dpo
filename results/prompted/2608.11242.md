Curator's take:  
Side Constraints (SCs) are user‑issued rules that must persist across turns, but current compaction methods often drop them, leading to violations. Zhiqi Wang et al. study this problem, quantify SC loss with the C ON PACT suite, and propose an SC extractor that keeps constraints separate from the task summary to preserve them.

Key points:  
**SC loss.** Compactors retain only 17 % of injected SCs on average, with the best case 28.6 % on naturally occurring SCs.  
**Scenario impact.** Retention varies by compactor, context length, SC framing, and injection location across multi‑turn chat, tool use, and long‑horizon research.  
**Extractor performance.** The SC extractor achieves over 90 % retention and 93.4–96.3 % downstream compliance across all three scenarios without modifying the compactor or LLM.  
**Why it matters.** Preserving SCs is essential for reliable LLM‑based systems, as compaction can silently remove constraints that govern user‑desired behavior.
