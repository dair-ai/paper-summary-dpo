Curator's take:  
Chao Zhang et al. study how to give writers proactive, higher‑level AI support by letting users create customizable “thought partners” that intervene at chosen moments. They build a technology probe that lets writers configure partner roles and timing, then evaluate it with 16 participants over a week.

Key points:  
**Customization.** Participants used the probe to translate anticipated needs into partner roles and timing conditions, treating configuration as prospective planning.  
**Engagement.** Users often ignored interventions to preserve flow, but when they engaged, suggestions helped both idea generation and self‑monitoring.  
**Representation.** Proactive support was perceived as less intrusive when delivered through lightweight visual cues and nondirective rhetorical framing.  
**Why it matters.** The study expands proactive writing assistance beyond local text help to higher‑level cognitive support, offering a new design space for personalized, timely AI partners.
