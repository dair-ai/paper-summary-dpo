**Curator's take**  
JIT‑Agent tackles the problem that an agent’s performance depends as much on its harness as on its foundation model. First‑author et al. propose a meta‑agent that, at inference time, generates a task‑specific harness for any off‑the‑shelf LLM, then repairs and evolves it using execution feedback.

**Key points**

- **Adaptivity.** JIT‑Agent receives a task specification, a protocol, a tool/skill registry, and prior harness context, and emits an executable harness tailored to the task.  
- **Reliability.** Stage II of training turns failed generations into bounded repair trajectories, converting compiler errors, interface mismatches, and runtime failures into supervision for reliable recovery.  
- **Evolvability.** Stage III introduces Evolutionary Group‑Decoupled Policy Optimization (Evo‑GDPO), which optimizes JIT‑Agent to propose harnesses that overtake the current archive frontier while normalizing reward, latency, and cost.  
- **Performance gains.** With JIT‑Agent, DeepSeek‑V4‑Flash surpasses GPT‑5.6 by 9.1 points on DeepSearchQA and 4.3 on OdysseyBench, while GLM‑5.2 gains up to 20.2 points.  
- **Why it matters.** JIT‑Agent is the first model purpose‑built for just‑in‑time harness generation, establishing harness intelligence as a trainable, transferable, and compounding dimension of agent capability orthogonal to model scaling.
